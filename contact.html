FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

Check-in is from 3:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out are available upon request, subject to availability. Guests who book directly with us receive complimentary early check-in when available.
Breakfast is complimentary for all guests who book directly through our website. Guests arriving via third-party booking platforms may add breakfast at a supplement. Our breakfast is served daily from 6:30 AM to 11:00 AM in the Terrace Restaurant.
Yes, we offer private airport transfers to and from JFK, LaGuardia, and Newark airports. Please contact our concierge team at least 48 hours in advance to arrange your transfer. Pricing varies by airport and vehicle class.
We offer valet parking for all guests. Self-parking is also available in our private underground garage. Parking fees apply and can be added to your room account. Please note spaces are limited — we recommend notifying us in advance.
Our standard rate allows free cancellation up to 48 hours before arrival. Cancellations made within 48 hours are subject to a one-night charge. Non-refundable rates are also available at a reduced price. Please refer to your booking confirmation for the specific terms that apply to your reservation.
We welcome well-behaved dogs up to 25 lbs in our pet-friendly rooms. A nightly pet fee applies. We provide a welcome amenity including a bed, bowl, and treats. Please inform us at the time of booking so we can prepare your room accordingly.
