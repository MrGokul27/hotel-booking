About This Suite

The Crown of Stackly

The Presidential Suite occupies an entire floor of Stackly, commanding unobstructed 360° views across Manhattan, the Brooklyn waterfront and beyond. At 220 square metres, it is not merely a room — it is an address in its own right. Two king bedrooms, a grand reception room, a formal dining room for eight, a private kitchen and a dedicated home cinema are yours for the duration of your stay.

Step onto your private rooftop terrace where a heated jacuzzi awaits beneath an open sky. A dedicated butler is available around the clock, anticipating your every need with discreet, unhurried precision. Champagne is chilled on arrival. Your preferences are noted before you arrive. From the moment you step in, the only thing left to do is enjoy.
