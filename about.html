Our Story

A Brooklyn
Institution Since 1999

"We didn't set out to build a hotel. We set out to build a feeling." — Marcus Webb, Founder

What began as a single converted brownstone on Evergreen Terrace grew over twenty-five years into Brooklyn's most celebrated luxury address. Every expansion, every renovation, every new hire has been guided by a single conviction: the finest hospitality is invisible — guests simply feel taken care of.

Today Stackly occupies an entire city block, yet retains the warmth and intimacy of that original brownstone. Each of our 120 rooms is unique, each corridor tells a chapter of Brooklyn's story, and each member of our 200-strong team carries the same founding spirit.

Our Journey

Milestones That Shaped Us

1999
The First Door Opens

Marcus Webb converts a 19th-century Brooklyn brownstone into a 12-room boutique hotel, opening on a rainy November morning.

2007
The Grand Expansion

Three adjoining buildings are acquired and Stackly grows to 60 rooms. The rooftop infinity pool and signature restaurant debut.

2015
Forbes Five-Star Recognition

Stackly earns its first Forbes Travel Guide Five-Star Award — the only independent hotel in Brooklyn to do so.

2024
A New Chapter

120 rooms, a world-class spa, two restaurants and 25 years of stories. Stackly enters its next era — bolder and more personal than ever.
