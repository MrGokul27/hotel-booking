About This Suite

Space Is Its Own Luxury

At 68 square metres, the Luxury Suite offers a world within itself. The generous bedroom flows into a separate sitting room with a bespoke sofa, wet bar and hand-laid herringbone parquet. A wraparound private terrace with alfresco dining for two captures Manhattan across the water from every angle.

In the bathroom, a freestanding copper bath sits centre stage beneath a skylight — beside a walk-in rain shower finished in Carrara marble, heated floors and illuminated vanity mirrors. Every surface, every material and every amenity has been chosen to feel deliberate and indulgent.
